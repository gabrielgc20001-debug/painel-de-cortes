Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Painel de Cortes ("the app"), a personal tool that edits short gameplay clips and publishes them to a TikTok account, handles information.

1. Information the app accesses

When the user connects a TikTok account, the app only requests:

  • Basic profile information (such as open ID, display name and avatar), used to show which account is connected.
  • Permission to publish videos, used to upload and post the clips the user chooses to the user's own TikTok account.

The app does not access messages, contacts, followers' data or any other information.

2. Where data is stored

The access tokens provided by TikTok are stored only on the user's own computer. They are not sent to or stored on any external server operated by the app.

3. No selling or sharing

No data is sold, rented or shared with third parties. Information is used only to perform the actions the user requests within the app.

4. Disconnecting your account

The user can disconnect the TikTok account at any time, either in the app or by removing the app's access in the TikTok account settings. When the account is disconnected, the stored tokens are deleted from the user's computer.

5. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
